# Image Observation Field Catalog

**Status:** P2 consistency review is complete under the two-party model in [`../P2_REVIEW_RECORD.md`](../P2_REVIEW_RECORD.md). Role ownership is specified, but operational role appointments remain open for later gates. This catalog is normative with [`image_observation.schema.json`](image_observation.schema.json), [`RUNTIME_INVARIANTS.md`](RUNTIME_INVARIANTS.md), and the downstream ENCODER convention. “Per observation” means immutable after publication. Referenced payload lifetime is controlled by the explicit retention/access policy, not by the record itself.

## Common payload-reference fields

Every payload reference carries a scheme, immutable resolver identity, content kind, positive byte length, SHA-256 digest, read-only access mode, access-policy ID, and retention declaration. Numeric payload kinds additionally carry positive `shape`, `dtype`, `layout`, and byte order. Numeric arrays use exact checked element-count × element-size byte lengths and a declared tight layout; padding, strides, or alternate encodings require a separately versioned content contract. Resolvers must bind the returned bytes to the reference identity and digest, reject mutable aliases, and enforce authorization and expiry before bytes are read.

The catalog describes producer and role ownership, not actual named appointments. The real store/access policy, source provenance, consumer, calibration authority, enabled features, and production profile remain owner decisions. The example records in [`examples/`](examples/) are synthetic and have no production retention or authority.
